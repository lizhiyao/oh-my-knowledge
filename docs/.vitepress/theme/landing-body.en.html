Connect evidence to changes, with explicit boundaries

Knowledge items are facts, experiences, or methods; artifacts are the prompts, documents, or skills carrying them. Extraction produces content for review; evaluation follows when you prepare an artifact change.

Observe

Inspect tasks, sources, and coverage.

Measure

Fix conditions and test whether a change helps.

Know

Preserve the evidence behind adoption, rollback, or further improvement.

Assess the evidence

Check the evidence and its scope before making a version decision.

01
How large is the difference?
Inspect differences and intervals
Look beyond a point estimate at sample size, comparison families, and uncertainty.
02
What supports the score?
Inspect cases and scoring evidence
Check criteria, coverage, and failures. Gold comparison can assess human/judge agreement.
03
Where does the conclusion apply?
Keep measurement and observation distinct
Conclusions depend on cases and conditions. Observation signals are not causal conclusions; generated cases need human review.
